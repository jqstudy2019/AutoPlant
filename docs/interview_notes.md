# Voice of Customer — Interview Notes

Goal: confirm or change the provisional user needs (UN-09, UN-12) and find needs we missed. Talk to 2–3 people who keep houseplants. 15 minutes each. Ask about their experience, not about the design.

## Questions

1. Tell me about the last time a plant of yours died or struggled. What happened?
2. How do you currently remember to water? What goes wrong?
3. When you're away for a few days, what do you do about your plants?
4. Where do your plants sit? How much space is around them?
5. If something watered your plants for you, what would worry you about it?
6. How would you want to know it needs attention (refill, problem)?
7. Who else in your home would deal with it?
