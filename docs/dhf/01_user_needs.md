# 01 — User Needs

Rev A (draft) · 2026-10-18

User needs are written in the user's language and are not directly testable; they are confirmed by validation (VAL-xx). Primary user: a home plant owner (the author) who travels or forgets to water. Secondary users: household members who refill the reservoir.

**Source:** author's own use, plus Voice of Customer interviews still to be done (see `../interview_notes.md`). Needs marked *provisional* will be revisited after interviews.

| ID | User need | Priority | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| UN-01 | My plant gets watered when its soil is dry, even when I'm not home. | Must | Author |
| UN-02 | My plant is never overwatered or flooded. | Must | Author |
| UN-03 | I'm told when the water tank needs refilling, before it runs out. | Must | Author |
| UN-04 | I can check how my plant is doing, now and over time, from my phone — at home or away. | Must | Author |
| UN-05 | I can water the plant on demand from my phone. | Should | Author |
| UN-06 | The system doesn't damage my home or hurt anyone (no leaks, no electrical hazard). | Must | Author |
| UN-07 | I can see the plant remotely. | Should | Author |
| UN-08 | It runs for at least two weeks without me touching it. | Must | Author |
| UN-09 | Refilling and setup are quick and need no tools. | Should | Provisional |
| UN-10 | I can add more planters later without starting over. | Should | Author |
| UN-11 | Only I (and people I choose) can see or control it remotely. | Must | Author |
| UN-12 | It fits on a windowsill or shelf. | Should | Provisional |
